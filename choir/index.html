Minichord Choir

Schola cantorum ex chorda una

One chord button, four instruments. Each voice of the chord arrives on its own MPE channel, and each channel plays its own timbre, glides included.

Minichord Lab · Open Sound Lab

You'll need a minichord running the unofficial test-allFeatures firmware (flash firmware.hex with the Teensy Loader), and Chrome, Edge or Opera. No minichord? The test buttons at the bottom play without one.

Press start, then allow MIDI access when the browser asks. Allowing system-exclusive messages lets this page switch MPE output on the minichord.

The voice leading demo plays the same eight chords twice, voiced exactly as the minichord voices them: first built up from each root, then voice-led.

The minichord is Benjamin Poilve's open-source instrument. MPE output, voice leading and the temperaments come from the unofficial test-allFeatures firmware; its settings editor is Sound Lab.
