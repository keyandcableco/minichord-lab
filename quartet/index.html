Minichord Lab

String quartet

The minichord's four chord voices, played by a string quartet. The lowest voice goes to the cello, the next to the viola, and the top two to the second and first violins. Each part shows the string it would play on. Click a player to rest them.

Connect the minichord, or take your seats and try the test chords below.

The browser needs a click before it plays anything. The players' recordings load once you do.

Each part on its own staff, first violin at the top, the viola in its alto clef and the cello in bass clef, in your minichord's key signature. A note is written as it starts and drawn out as long as it lasts; a glide bends its line.

Re-voice

Changes here are live on the minichord and last until the preset changes; save the preset in Sound Lab to keep them. With MPE each part follows one voice, so voice leading and glide show as each player's line moving on the score. Slash voice and cantus choose which player takes a slash note or the harp's note.

How they play

Articulation

Dynamic

From a dry studio to a stone church.

Light draws the score at a quarter of the frame rate and at plain resolution, keeps the bows still, and uses a shorter hall. The sound is the same.

The parts

Seating

In range, a part asked for a note below its lowest open string plays it an octave up, as a player would. An octave-down cello gives the bass the weight of a larger group.
