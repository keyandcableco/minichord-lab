Minichord Lab

Looper

Record the minichord in layers, to a click: its sound over USB audio and its MIDI at the same time. Loop it, overdub it, stack takes on tracks, move them around, and take it all away as WAV, stems or MIDI.

Connect the minichord, then start the looper.

The browser asks to use an audio input: choose the minichord (it's a USB sound card as well as a MIDI device). Nothing leaves this computer; takes are kept in this browser until you delete them.

Clip

Sound in and out

With the minichord's USB audio set to play the computer's sound (setting 244 to 1), the tracks and the click come out of the minichord, beside what you play, and none of it is recorded twice: the minichord sends only its own sound back.

So its rhythm mode follows the looper's tempo, starting with it.

Plays eight clicks: pluck a harp string on each (or play a short chord), letting each die away. Compares when each note's MIDI arrived with when its sound did. A sound with a quick attack measures best; the latency can shift a few milliseconds each time the input opens, so calibrate again if takes start to sit off the beat.

If the takes sit late against the click, move them earlier here.

From the minichord, or a foot

Two quick taps of the modifier then take the next step, record, play, stop, placed where the second tap landed (double tap control 256, value 7; firmware 37 on). Any MIDI controller works it too: control change 85 records, 86 plays, 87 stops, 88 undoes, 89 switches overdubbing, 90 takes the next step, as the minichord's own looper takes them.

Songs

    Take it away

    The project file holds the song and every take, to open on another computer.

    Keys: Space play or stop, R record, Home to the start, T tap tempo, C click on or off, L loop on or off, 1–9 arm a track, N new track, Ctrl+Z undo, Delete the clip, Ctrl+D duplicate it, S split it at the playhead, ← → nudge it.

    A track plays its takes' own sound, or their MIDI: through the browser's piano, through the minichord (it then needs no USB audio, but has its sixteen voices, and what you play live takes them back), or through any MIDI device. A track played through the minichord is heard in what it records, so mute it while recording over it.

    Needs Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer or Android, and the minichord's USB audio (firmware 24 on). Without USB audio, takes keep the MIDI only.
