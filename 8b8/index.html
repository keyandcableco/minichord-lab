8b8 Link

The minichord playing the 8-Bit 8asterd: three AY-3-8910 sound chips, on the board or in its emulator. Its chords and strings play the chips, in the minichord's own tuning, its knobs turn the 8b8's settings, and its rhythm mode keeps time with an 8b8 drum pattern.

The 8b8

Not connected. Over USB the settings go through the 8b8's serial port, so close its own control panel first; Chrome or Edge on a computer.

What plays what

On drums each string hits a piece of the 8b8's kit, lowest string the bass drum. With exact tuning on, every string has its own channel, so a string is always the same drum; without it, the note's letter picks the drum.

Tuning

The 8b8 follows the minichord's temperament and its A.

Voicing on the minichord

With voice leading on, the minichord's four voices arrive bottom to top: bass, tenor, alto, soprano, each moving as little as it can. Cantus lets the harp set one voice's note; slash voice lets a slash chord set one. The minichord's glide arrives as bends, carried by the latched voices as they move; the 8b8's own Glide, in its sound, slides each latched voice from its own last note. Use one or the other. The 8b8's Buzzy Bass always takes its lowest note and its wavetable its highest, so latched, those are the bass line and the soprano line.

Tracker

The 8b8's nine channels as a tracker shows them, read from its chips' registers: a scope for each channel, three to a chip, and rows scrolling up with each new note, volume, slide and note let go, the drums by name, and each chip's noise and envelope. The scopes are rebuilt from the registers with the same model of the chip the emulator runs.

Knobs

The minichord sends its three knobs while the page is open, and each can turn any of the 8b8's settings across its whole range. The chord and harp knobs also set how hard the minichord plays, so they are the 8b8's loudness too; the modulation knob is free.

Sounds and presets

Each of the minichord's presets can keep an 8b8 sound: load the preset on the minichord and its sound loads on the 8b8.

Clock and drums

The page keeps time: a drum pattern on the 8b8, and MIDI clock to the minichord. Put the minichord in rhythm mode (a long press of the hold button) and its chord pattern locks to the drums, starting together on Play.

What the 8b8 says

Part of Minichord Lab. The 8b8 is the 8-Bit 8asterd firmware; its emulator runs that firmware itself, compiled for the browser. Exact tuning, MPE, the knobs and the clock come from the unofficial test-allFeatures firmware for the minichord.
