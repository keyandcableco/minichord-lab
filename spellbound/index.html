Settings

Chord sets follow what the buttons play: standard is major, minor, 7, maj7, m7, diminished (major and minor together) and augmented (all three); Barry Harris mode turns major, minor and diminished into 6, m6 and diminished 7. The alternate layout is left out, since everyone sets up their own. The buttons play their letters with the key signature's sharps or flats, and the game sets the modifier to sharp or flat for each question.

Part of Minichord Lab. The minichord is Benjamin Poilve's open-source instrument; key signatures, the modifier setting, harp scales, slash settings, temperaments and MPE come from the unofficial test-allFeatures firmware. Source on GitHub.
